    01 / Business Problem

    One portfolio. Four different performance realities.

    Store performance cannot be managed through sales alone. A store may achieve its sales benchmark while underperforming on GP3, or protect margin while still missing its sales opportunity. Without a combined view, high-potential stores and stores requiring intervention can be treated with the same action plan.

    Core questionWhich stores are winning on both sales and profitability, which have untapped potential, and which need a focused recovery plan?
    02 / Quadrant Framework

    Sales × GP3: a two-dimensional view of store health

    Each store is positioned using its sales achievement against an approved store benchmark and its GP3 margin. This separates sales performance from profitability instead of allowing one metric to hide the other.

    STARSSales ≥ 100% · GP3 ≥ 15%

    Strong sales and healthy profitability. Protect, sustain and replicate.

    BLOOMERSSales < 100% · GP3 ≥ 15%

    Healthy margin with sales upside. Accelerate growth without diluting GP3.

    MATURESSales ≥ 100% · GP3 < 15%

    Sales are delivered, but margin needs attention. Focus on cost and mix.

    LAGGARDSSales < 100% · GP3 < 15%

    Both dimensions need improvement. Prioritize root-cause diagnosis and recovery.

    03 / Data & Metrics

    Make stores comparable before classifying them

    Sales AchievementActual Sales ÷ Store Sales Benchmark × 100%Measures how the store performs against its sales benchmark for the selected review period.
    GP3 MarginGP3 ÷ Net Sales × 100%Measures profitability relative to net sales, using a consistent GP3 definition.
    Store DimensionStore / Code GIS · RM · AM · City · ProvinceProvides stable store mapping and supports drill-down by operational responsibility.
    Period & MaturityMonth · Operating days · GO dateSupports like-for-like comparison and helps interpret newly opened stores separately when needed.
    04 / Analytical Workflow

    From store-level data to management prioritization

    01Set the review baseline

    Choose a comparable period and confirm the sales benchmark, GP3 definition and store inclusion rules.

    02Calculate store KPIs

    Calculate sales achievement and GP3 margin at one row per store and review period.

    03Assign quadrant

    Apply both thresholds consistently to classify each store into one of four buckets.

    04Find the performance pattern

    Compare bucket composition by region, RM, AM, city and store maturity to identify concentration and gaps.

    05Track MTM movement

    Match stores across consecutive months and classify transitions as improving, stable or declining.

    06Prioritize action

    Translate the quadrant and movement analysis into differentiated store action plans and follow-up.

    05 / Strategic Action Plan

    Different buckets require different management responses

    STARS · Sustain & Replicate
    • Protect service, product quality and store execution.
    • Identify practices that can be replicated by other stores.
    • Monitor whether sales growth is achieved without GP3 dilution.
    BLOOMERS · Unlock Sales Potential
    • Review traffic, transactions and average check opportunities.
    • Improve local activation, product availability and upselling.
    • Grow sales while protecting the healthy GP3 margin.
    MATURES · Recover Profitability
    • Review food cost, discounts, waste and product mix.
    • Assess manpower productivity and controllable operating costs.
    • Set GP3 recovery actions while maintaining sales momentum.
    LAGGARDS · Focused Recovery
    • Diagnose root causes across sales drivers and cost structure.
    • Set store-specific sales and GP3 recovery priorities.
    • Apply closer follow-up with clear owners and review cadence.
    06 / Month-to-Month Movement

    Performance is a journey, not a static label

    The transition matrix follows each store from its baseline bucket to its latest bucket. It distinguishes stores that sustain performance from those improving, slipping or remaining in the same segment.

    How to read the matrix

    Rows represent the starting bucket; columns represent the latest bucket. Diagonal values are stores that stayed in the same bucket. Off-diagonal values show movement between performance segments.
